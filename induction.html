Mandatory PPE

  • Hi-Vis Clothing – worn at all times on site.
  • Safety Boots – approved steel-cap footwear for the task.
  • Hard Hats – worn during overhead work (cranes, concrete pumps, lifting operations) and wherever signed.
  • Task-Specific PPE – eye, hearing and other PPE as required by your SWMS or site signage.
  • Sun Safety – no singlets; apply 30+ sunscreen every 2 hours.

Conduct

  • Drugs & Alcohol – none on site; anyone affected will be removed.
  • Smoking – in designated areas only; not in offices, sheds or amenities.
  • Behaviour – no conduct that risks safety or harasses others.
  • Fatigue – keep to normal hours and breaks; raise any concerns.
  • Prohibited – no animals, personal headphones/radios, or under-18s (except approved apprentices).

Hazards, Injuries & Emergency

  • Report Hazards – report unsafe conditions to your supervisor or Site Manager immediately.
  • Injuries – report to First Aid and log in the injury register.
  • Emergency – know the alarm and assembly point (see the site plan below).
  • Safety Signs – obey all signs; never remove or damage them.

On-Site Operations

  • Sign On / Off – scan the QR poster every time you enter and leave site.
  • SWMS – sign into your SWMS before any high-risk work.
  • Electrical – RCDs tested daily; leads off the ground; test & tag current; max 30 m lead run.
  • Permits – high-risk tasks (electrical isolation, confined space, hot works, excavation, etc.) need a permit — your supervisor arranges it.
  • Movement & Access – use designated walkways; beware mobile plant; do not enter barricaded areas.
  • Parking – only by arrangement with Site Management; park per ACT road rules.
  • Heights & Falling Objects – protect work at height; secure tools and materials; toe boards on scaffold.
  • Chemicals – an SDS for every chemical; work to the SWMS and SDS.
  • Housekeeping – clean up as you go; store in designated areas; keep walkways clear.
  • Visitors – report to the site office, sign in, and stay escorted by an inducted person.

Safety Breaches

  • Immediate removal – tampering with safety equipment, fighting, drugs or alcohol, theft, harassment or vandalism may result in immediate removal and possible prosecution.
  • Warning system – minor breaches: verbal warning → written warning → removal from site.
