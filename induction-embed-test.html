Site Safety Rules

Elm Building Group — Annexure D Site Safety Rules · Version: <<CONFIRM-RULES-VERSION>>

These general rules apply to every person who enters this work site.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Hard hats (AS 1801) worn at all times on site, unless a documented risk assessment alters this.
  • High-visibility clothing worn at all times on site.
  • Approved safety footwear appropriate to the task.
  • It is each person's responsibility to use and maintain the correct PPE, tools and equipment.
  • Loose clothing, loose hair and jewellery must not be worn while working.

Sun Safety

  • Practise sun safety outdoors. Singlets are not permitted. Apply 30+ broad-spectrum sunscreen every 2 hours.

Conduct & Work Practice

  • Work to ensure your own and others' health and safety. No behaviour likely to cause incident or injury, and none that offends, upsets or harasses the client, public or other workers.
  • Comply with WHS legislation, codes of practice, the Elm Building Group PMP, manufacturers' instructions, approved SWMS, and all lawful supervisor instructions.

Drugs, Alcohol, Smoking & Prohibited Items

  • No drugs or alcohol on site — anyone appearing under the influence will be removed.
  • Observe smoke-free zones; no smoking in offices, sheds or amenities.
  • No animals on site at any time.
  • No personal headphones/radios; AM/FM radios only at the Site Manager's discretion.
  • No persons under 18 unless on a formal apprenticeship or approved work experience.

Hazards, Injuries & Reporting

  • If an unsafe condition needs urgent attention, correct it if safe to do so; otherwise make it as safe as possible and report it immediately.
  • Report all unsafe conditions or practices to your supervisor or the Elm PM/Site Manager. Use the Site Daily Notice Board to communicate hazards.
  • Report all injuries to the First Aid Facility and log them in the project Register of Injuries (failure to notify may affect your workers' compensation cover).

Emergency & Evacuation

  • Know the site emergency procedures. Familiarise yourself with the assembly area and evacuation system — see the Site Plan below. Each person must know the evacuation signal and assembly point.

Electrical

  • Electrical isolation and energisation permits are in use on this site.
  • RCDs MUST be used — push-button tested daily. Keep leads on stands/hooks — no leads on the ground. Maximum 30 m from the temporary distribution board to the tool. Testing and tagging must be current.

Fatigue

  • Maintain normal hours and breaks to avoid fatigue. Inform your supervisor or the Elm PM/Site Manager of any fatigue concerns.

Site Access, Parking & Movement

  • Vehicles on site only by prior arrangement with Site Management; park per ACT road rules. Elm accepts no responsibility for subcontractor vehicles.
  • Use designated, clearly defined access; emergency access is per the Emergency Evacuation Plan.
  • Use designated walkways. Do not leave site without informing your supervisor (you may need to be accounted for in an emergency). Beware mobile plant, cranes and vehicles; obey dogman/rigger/operator directions; do not enter barrier-controlled areas unless authorised.

Housekeeping & Signs

  • Keep the site clean and free of waste; store materials safely in designated locations; keep all exits, walkways and stairwells clear.
  • Observe all safety signs. Do not remove, shift or destroy any warning sign without Elm authorisation; report accidental removal immediately.

Working at Heights & Falling Objects

  • Wear a hard hat in hard-hat areas; stay clear of no-go areas and slung/lifted loads; secure loose materials; do not stack near unmeshed guardrails or edges; use tool lanyards at height; ensure scaffold toe boards are in place; isolate areas below work areas.

Hazardous Substances & Chemicals

  • Always refer to the SDS and work in accordance with the approved SWMS and applicable SDS.
  • All chemicals must have an SDS; containers clearly marked with contents and safe-handling procedures; store per manufacturer/SDS; liaise with Site Management on storage.

Specific Activities

  • Core cutting / excavating: obtain Elm permission before starting and comply with any conditions.
  • Compressed air: do not skylark or use it to blow dust from hands, hair or clothing.
  • Ladders: platform ladders only — a ladder risk assessment is required if a platform ladder is not practicable.

Visitors & Public Protection

  • Visitors report to the site office, sign the visitors register, and are accompanied at all times by an inducted person.
  • The public is protected by perimeter fencing/hoarding, barricades/para-webbing and signage as the PM/Site Manager deems necessary.
  • Report any security concerns to Site Management; gates are locked after hours (Elm management holds the only keys).

Safety Breaches — immediate removal

Any person found in breach of the following may be removed from site without warning:

  • Removing handrails, guardrails, barricades or fences without authority; fighting or violence; bullying or pranks; refusing to comply with safety instructions; interfering with, damaging or vandalising safety equipment or amenities; attending under the influence of drugs or alcohol; racial vilification; sexual harassment; urinating or defecating outside the toilets provided; theft.

Minor non-conformance follows a warning system: 1) Verbal warning → 2) Written warning (Safety Improvement Notice) → 3) Removal from site. Any serious breach results in immediate removal and may result in prosecution.
