Site Safety Rules

Elm Building Group — Annexure D Site Safety Rules · Version: <<CONFIRM-RULES-VERSION>>

Mandatory PPE

Hard HatsWorn while working on site (AS 1801).
Hi-Vis ClothingWorn at all times on site.
Safety BootsApproved steel-cap footwear for the task.
Task PPEEye, hearing and other PPE as required by SWMS/signage.
Sun SafetyNo singlets. Apply 30+ sunscreen every 2 hours.

Conduct

Drugs & AlcoholNone on site — anyone affected is removed.
SmokingSmoke-free zones; not in offices, sheds or amenities.
BehaviourNo conduct that risks safety or harasses others.
FatigueKeep to normal hours and breaks; raise any concerns.
ProhibitedNo animals, personal radios/headphones, or under-18s (except approved apprentices).

Hazards, Injuries & Emergency

Report HazardsReport unsafe conditions to your supervisor/Site Manager immediately.
InjuriesReport to First Aid and log in the injury register.
EmergencyKnow the alarm and assembly point — see the site plan below.
Safety SignsObey all signs; never remove or damage them.

On-Site Operations

Sign On / OffScan the QR poster every time. Be inducted + SWMS-signed before access.
SWMSMandatory for high-risk work — sign into your SWMS before starting.
ElectricalRCDs tested daily; leads off the ground; test & tag current; max 30 m lead run.
PermitsRequired for high-risk tasks (isolation, confined space, hot works, excavation, etc.) — supervisor arranges.
Movement & AccessUse designated walkways; beware mobile plant; don't enter barricaded areas.
ParkingOnly by arrangement with Site Management; park per ACT road rules.
Heights & Falling ObjectsProtect work at height; secure tools/materials; toe boards on scaffold.
ChemicalsAn SDS for every chemical; work to the SWMS and SDS.
HousekeepingClean up progressively; store in designated areas; keep walkways clear.
VisitorsReport to the site office, sign in, and stay escorted by an inducted person.

Safety Breaches

Immediate removalRemoving safety equipment, fighting, drugs/alcohol, theft, harassment, vandalism or similar may result in immediate removal and possible prosecution.
Warning systemMinor breaches: verbal warning → written warning → removal from site.
